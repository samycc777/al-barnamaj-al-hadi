# Scholars' and authors' death dates (checked)

The rule is in CLAUDE.md (Conventions). These are the dates already checked, moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md:

- Dates checked so far: al-Jabiri 1444, Rabee 1447, al-Adeni 1439, Ibn Baz 1420, Zayd al-Madkhali 1435, al-Faqihi 1446, al-Jami 1416, al-Albani 1420 (22 Jumada al-Akhirah 1420, Arabic Wikipedia), Abd al-Aziz Al al-Shaykh 1447 (1 Rabi' al-Akhir 1447 = 23 Sep 2025, Royal Court via SPA, Al Arabiya), al-Qusayr 1445, Ibn Uthaymeen 1421, al-Ityubi 1442, al-Najmi 1429, al-Luhaydan 1443, al-Arfaj 1442, Ali al-Wusaifi 1444 (Egyptian news, 26 Ramadan 1444); Muqbil al-Wadi'i 1422 (30 Rabi' al-Akhir 1422, dorar.net / Wikipedia); Muhammad Taqi al-Din al-Hilali 1407 (25 Shawwal 1407, Casablanca, Arabic Wikipedia); Abdullah bin Abd al-Rahman al-Ghudayan 1431 (18 Jumada al-Akhirah 1431 = 1 June 2010, SPA condolence / Arabic Wikipedia); alive: al-Fawzan, al-Abbad, al-Hudhaifi, Kouni, Salih al-Hudaythi; Fallatah 1419 (not on the site); Abd al-Rahman ibn Hasan Al al-Shaykh (author of فتح المجيد and قرة عيون الموحدين) 1285 (footnote in al-Fawzan's printed شرح فتح المجيد, citing مشاهير علماء نجد); Muhammad bin Ibrahim Al al-Shaykh (Grand Mufti, شرح الواسطية) 1389 (24 Ramadan 1389, Arabic Wikipedia).
- Less-known authors checked (Arabic Wikipedia / al-Zirikli): al-Bayquni «نحو 1080» (exact year unknown), al-Zanjani 655, al-Bunjawi 1336, Bahraq al-Hadrami (the additions to لامية الأفعال) 930; Dr. Mahdi Rizqallah Ahmad (born 1945) has no death report, so no date.
- سعيد بن علي بن وهف القحطاني: 21 Muharram 1440 (alyaum.com, al-madina.com news reports).
- ابن أبي العز الحنفي 792 (Arabic Wikipedia, al-Zirikli).
- أبو جعفر الطحاوي 321 (Arabic Wikipedia, al-Dhahabi's Siyar).
- محمد خليل هراس (author of the شرح العقيدة الواسطية al-Jami comments on) 1395 (1334–1395 هـ = 1916–1975, ketabonline / al-Zirikli).
