# CLAUDE.md

البرنامج الهادي لتأصيل العلم: an Arabic (RTL) Islamic study site whose lessons are grouped into ten categories (العقيدة، آداب الطالب، الحديث، علوم الحديث، الفقه، أصول وقواعد الفقه، التفسير، أصول وقواعد التفسير، السيرة، علوم العربية). Its look follows rabee.net: cream parchment with a geometric pattern, a brown/gold nav bar, panels with brown headers and round gold badges, Tajawal for text, Aref Ruqaa for the site name, Amiri for Qur'an and ḥadīth. The user adds content little by little.

Live at https://al-barnamaj-al-hadi.pages.dev (Cloudflare Pages project `al-barnamaj-al-hadi`, linked to the GitHub repo `samycc777/al-barnamaj-al-hadi`: every push to `main` rebuilds and deploys it).

## Layout

- `src/content.mjs`: all content. `site` (name, url) and `categories`, each with `items` (lessons: `title`, `type` audio/video/pdf/article/link, `url`, `date`, `note`). A single recorded lecture gets `audio` (its mp3) and `download` instead of `url`, and plays right on the category page with the same player bar as book pages; never just link out to the Shaykh's page when there is an mp3. To add a lesson, add an item there. A book with many recorded lessons gets `slug` and `source` (its lessons page on the Shaykh's site, al-abbaad.com) instead of `url`, and its own page at `/<category>/<slug>/`. A book from another site (miraath.net, for scholars with no official site) uses that site's page as `source`. The source is only for the importer; the site does not show it (the user asked for no source link). When the source site files a lesson under the wrong book, list its number in the book's `skip` so the importer leaves it out.
- A category can group its books into `sections` (`{ title, items }`), shown on the category page as foldable groups that start closed (the programme's manager asked for this): العقيدة has مؤلفات السلف, مؤلفات الإمام محمد بن عبد الوهاب and مؤلفات ابن تيمية, علوم العربية has النحو, الصرف and البلاغة, آداب الطالب has الدعوة (after its own books). Book pages stay at `/<category>/<slug>/`. Code that walks all books uses `itemsOf(c)` from `content.mjs`.
- Shaykh al-Fawzan's official site (alfawzan.af.org.sa) is suspended, so his books play from archive.org (mostly the مشروع كبار العلماء uploads, which include a lesson index showing how far each lesson goes). Al-Jami's books are on archive.org too. These lesson lists have no `source` and were written once by hand. His شرح كتاب الكبائر is incomplete everywhere (the newest series stops at باب تعظيم قتل النفس).
- `src/lessons/<slug>.json`: each book's chapters and lessons (title, date, audio address, download link, size), copied from the Shaykh's site (or miraath.net) by `npm run import`. The audio stays on his site; the book page plays it from there with one player bar at the bottom.
- `scripts/import-lessons.mjs`: the importer. Lessons the source lacks can be added by hand from another copy with `"extra": true`; the importer keeps them (in their chapter if the source has it). al-Abbad's صحيح البخاري uses this for lessons 284–624 (al-abbaad.com stops at 283; archive.org `Sharh_Sahih_al_Bukhari_al-Abbaad_2`, grouped into كتب by hand; checked 1 Oct 2026: the site's folders 55 كتاب الوصايا and 56 كتاب الجهاد والسير are empty and `/jdownloads/durus/hadith/shahih-albukhari/` holds only up to 283.mp3. Its «تكملات شرح صحيح البخاري (القديم)» page is a different thing: 44 lessons from 1434 that fill gaps in the old commentary, شرح صحيح البخاري (القديم), about 717 lessons from the 1420s–1434, which covers the whole book to كتاب التوحيد) and سنن أبي داود for lessons 1–2 (`Sharh_Sunan_Abee-Daawood`).
- تفسير السعدي is the complete reading by Shaykh Muhammad bin Ali al-Arfaj (IslamHouse), played from its archive.org copy (`tafsir_alsa3di_mp3`); its lesson list, one chapter per sūrah, was written once by hand from that item's file list.
- Ibn Uthaymeen's books (زاد المستقنع, الكافي) play straight from his audio server, sounds.binothaimeen.net, which allows skipping through. Their lesson lists have no `source` (the importer skips them) and were written once from binothaimeen.net's API: `https://shekhapi.binothaimeen.net/course/sections/audio_library/children/<section id>?pageSize=100` lists a book's parts, `.../many_lessons/<part id>/200` a part's lessons, each with `certificate_url` (the mp3's path on sounds.binothaimeen.net) and `objective.size`. When a book's section id is unknown, the mp3s can also be found by their path pattern, `othaimeen_K/<book>_<n>/<book>_<n>_othaimeen_K_<NN>.mp3`, where `<n>` is the recording (الشرح الأول / الثاني); شرح البلاغة (`albalaghah`) was found this way.
- Shaykh Rabee's lectures play straight from rabee.net (`https://rabee.net/wp-json/wp/v2/search?search=<title>` finds the audio page; its mp3 is under `wp-content/uploads/bulk-files/sounds/`). A lecture that rabee.net splits into several parts (مرحبا يا طالب العلم) is joined into one mp3 (`ffmpeg -f concat … -c copy`, the parts share one format) and played from our R2 bucket as a single lecture.
- Shaykh Muhammad Ali Adam al-Ityubi's books play straight from his official site, aletioupi.com (skippable mp3s under `site/wp-content/uploads/`). شرح مقدمة صحيح مسلم (قرة عين المحتاج, 36 lessons) has no `source`; its lesson list was written once from the two pages of its category (`/songs/الدروس-العلمية/مصطلح-الحديث/مقدمة-الإمام-مسلم/`). His one Arabic book left on the site, تصريف العزي (الصرف), comes from the categories under `/songs/الدروس-العلمية/اللغة-وعلومها/`, whose rows link either to mp3s on his site or to archive.org copies; it was written once by hand too. His ألفية ابن مالك, متن البناء, أرجوزة التصريف and لامية الأفعال were replaced by Kouni's commentaries (the manager asked, 1 Oct 2026; see below). شذور الذهب is not added because every copy online (his site, archive.org, islamweb) is an incomplete set of pieces with no clear order.
- Shaykh Abd al-Rahman bin Awf Kouni's النحو and الصرف books (الآجرومية, متممة الآجرومية, قطر الندى, ألفية ابن مالك, متن البناء, عنوان الظرف, لامية الأفعال) play from archive.org uploads (`xboxgamer-4518` for the 1435 الآجرومية, `sharh_almoqademah_al-aajroumiyah` for the 1439 one, `kawni-ajro-motamim`, `Konie_katrNada`, `kawni-alphiah`, `kawni-bina-asas-sarf`, `Sharh_3onwan_Dharf_Fi_3ilm_Sarf`, `04_20231127`); lesson lists written once by hand. Avoid `konie_ajjramiya`: its numbering is off by one and tape 1 is missing. In `kawni-alphiah`, file 42 is a copy of 41 (same audio), so the ألفية lacks lesson 42; file 54 is the خاتمة. `04_20231127` holds the original mp3s of the لامية الأفعال series also in `kawni-lamiah-ibnmalik` (7 tapes, numbered 00–06); `kawni-lamiah-ibnmalik-hazrami` is a different 5-tape course on the لامية with al-Hadrami's additions. متن البناء uses the 8-lesson series (archive.org, uploaded December 2014); a newer 12-lesson course, «الإيناس بشرح متن البناء والأساس» (YouTube only, 2022), is not used. عنوان الظرف (5 majalis, April 2017) comes from the YouTube channel «موقع دروس الكويت».
- Copied from islamweb or a flaky archive.org item into R2 (folders named after the book): al-Ityubi's نخبة الفكر (islamweb, 12 lessons; the folders `lamiyyat-al-afal-2` and `urjuzat-at-tasrif` still hold his لامية الأفعال and أرجوزة التصريف, no longer on the site). His تعليقات على الاختيارات الفقهية has no recording anywhere (printed book only), so it is not added until the programme decides.

Its page `/cha-<id>` lists the lessons; each plays from `https://www.mimham.net/audcha-<id>-<n>` (skippable, no `.mp3` ending, which `check.mjs` allows) and downloads from the row's `tan-…` link. Such a book (al-Jabiri's منظومة القواعد الفقهية) has no `source` and a lesson list written once from that page. Its lessons are numbered 1–8 in order: mimham.net's lesson 7 is left out because it is a copy of lesson 6 (the same copy is on archive.org and on Miraath's YouTube playlist, whose "lesson 7" is also lesson 6), so mimham's 8 shows as 7. mimham's 9 is not listed: it is al-Jabiri's شرح كشف الشبهات filed under this book (the poem's closing lines are explained near the end of mimham's 8, whose last minutes are the Kashf lesson's opening). al-Jabiri's الأمر بالمعروف والنهي عن المنكر also plays from mimham.net (`/cha-1428`, the whole course in 8 lessons, 7h20, دورة الإمام محمد بن إبراهيم بجدة 1429; `/cha-668` lists the same files). It replaced a 12-part archive.org copy (`mohamedabdlha446_gmail_20190614`, kept in R2 under `al-amr-bil-maruf-jabiri/`) that cuts the same sessions into halves with pauses trimmed and lacks parts 6, 9 and 10. The ajurry.com thread (node 31672, old t=30890) has the same 8 sessions as attachments, but numbers them in the wrong order (its tapes 4–8, 1–3 are mimham's 1–8, matched by length and audio).
- Audio that exists nowhere as playable mp3 files (التفسير الميسر with al-Hudhaifi's recitation, which is only on YouTube; the first Ibn Uthaymeen books, added before we found his audio server) is kept in the Cloudflare R2 bucket `al-barnamaj-al-hadi-media`, public at https://pub-04b2ff031f654021aecd7529acd87d9c.r2.dev. Such a book has `slug` but no `source`, and its `src/lessons/<slug>.json` is written by hand (the importer skips it). To add files: take the audio with `yt-dlp`, convert it to 64k mono mp3 with ffmpeg, check it has no music, then `npx wrangler r2 object put al-barnamaj-al-hadi-media/<slug>/<file>.mp3 --file <file>.mp3 --content-type audio/mpeg --content-disposition 'attachment; filename="<file>.mp3"' --remote`.
- `build.mjs`: builds static pages into `dist/` (home, one page per category at `/<slug>/`, one per book at `/<category>/<book>/`, `404.html`). No dependencies. Icons are inline SVG line icons in `ICONS`.
- `check.mjs`: checks every page was built, every internal link resolves, and every book has a lesson list.
- `assets/`: `style.css` (colour tokens at the top) and `favicon.svg`.

## Commands

- `npm run build`: build into `dist/`.
- `npm run check`: build and verify. This is the full check command; run it after every change and merge.
- `npm run dev`: build and serve on http://localhost:4321 (`scripts/dev.mjs`, no dependencies). It watches `src/`, `assets/` and `build.mjs`, rebuilds on every change and reloads the open page. Run in the project folder (main), it shows each change as soon as it is merged. If port 4321 is already in use it says so and exits.
- The preview is always running: the user wants `npm run dev` on at all times. A macOS LaunchAgent (`~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.samycachon.tasil-ilm-preview.plist`) runs `node scripts/dev.mjs` in the project folder at login and restarts it if it stops; its output goes to `~/Library/Logs/tasil-ilm-preview.log`. So don't start another `npm run dev` on port 4321 (it would only report that the port is in use). Restart it with `launchctl kickstart -k gui/$(id -u)/com.samycachon.tasil-ilm-preview`. It runs `/opt/homebrew/bin/node`; if node moves, update the plist.
- The user's cmux has a workspace command **Tasil Ilm preview** (in `~/.config/cmux/cmux.json`, run from the Command Palette ⌘⇧P): Claude on the left, the site at localhost:4321 on the right with the preview's log below it.
- `npm run import` (or `npm run import -- <slug>`): refresh books' lesson lists from al-abbaad.com. Run it after adding a book, or when the Shaykh's site adds lessons, then commit the JSON.

Cloudflare builds with `npm run build` and serves `dist/`. Deploying = pushing `main` to GitHub (a push needs the user's OK only if they haven't asked for the change to go live).

## Conventions

- Arabic, RTL, no framework. Keep pages static.
- Follow the user's religious content rules: no images of living beings or faces (icons are objects and shapes only), no music, and cite every āyah and ḥadīth (sūrah and number; ḥadīth with source and grading).
- Every audio lesson must be listenable directly on the website, with the site's own player bar. Never just link out (to YouTube, the Shaykh's site or anywhere else). Play the mp3 from where it is already hosted (al-abbaad.com, miraath.net, archive.org); if it exists only as a video (YouTube), take the audio, store it in our R2 bucket (see above) and play it from there.
- A scholar who has died gets his death year after his name in `note`, as «(ت 1421هـ)» (the programme's manager asked for this, 1 Oct 2026). Living scholars get nothing. Check a new scholar's date against a reliable source (news report, his site) before adding it. Dates checked so far: al-Jabiri 1444, Rabee 1447, al-Adeni 1439, Ibn Baz 1420, Zayd al-Madkhali 1435, al-Faqihi 1446, al-Jami 1416, al-Qusayr 1445, Ibn Uthaymeen 1421, al-Ityubi 1442, al-Luhaydan 1443, al-Arfaj 1442; alive: al-Fawzan, al-Abbad, al-Hudhaifi, Kouni.
- Put on the site only text the user gave. No taglines, verses, quotes, intro lines or "coming soon" messages of our own; only the words needed to use the site (menu labels, page not found).
